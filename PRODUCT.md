# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- Consumers searching for a home or investment opportunity across Buenos Aires, Córdoba, Rosario, Mar del Plata, and Mendoza. Their primary job is to describe what matters in natural language, inspect editable criteria, evaluate canonical properties with clear provenance, and delegate continuous monitoring without receiving noisy alerts.
- Private publishers who need a low-friction way to publish and manage a sale or rental opportunity, starting with only the essential facts and reviewing any AI-assisted enrichment before it becomes public.
- Real-estate operators who need a professional identity, manual and bulk publishing, eligible listing claims, availability management, analytics, and direct inquiries without losing source provenance.
- Internal administrators who review ambiguous duplicates, publisher verification, source health, failed jobs, and sensitive audit trails.

## Product Purpose

Agentic Real Estate is a mobile-first personal real-estate intelligence product. It turns user intent into persistent search agents, aggregates source publications into canonical property opportunities, ranks them by personal fit, tracks freshness and meaningful changes, and keeps every source visible. Success means people make better property decisions with less repetitive portal checking, less duplicate noise, and more confidence in what changed and why it matters.

The first release is for Argentina, focused on Buenos Aires, Córdoba, Rosario, Mar del Plata, and Mendoza. The product language is Spanish (Argentina), with ARS and USD treated as first-class currencies.

## Brand Personality

Calm, discerning, trustworthy.

The product should reduce the anxiety and repetitive work of property search. It communicates with measured confidence, explains the evidence behind conclusions, and stays candid when freshness, provenance, or AI confidence is uncertain.

## Anti-references

- A traditional property portal with a chatbot attached.
- A wall of filters as the first interaction.
- A generic SaaS dashboard aesthetic.
- Opaque AI that presents estimates as facts or prevents users from editing inferred criteria.
- Listing-centric presentation that hides duplicate publications, uncertainty, or provenance.
- Notification systems that alert on every update instead of meaningful changes.
- Lead-routing marketplaces that send an inquiry to unrelated agents by default.

## Design Principles

1. Start with intent, then expose editable structure.
2. Present the canonical property first, with source provenance always available.
3. Lead with conclusions: fit, concern, freshness, and price movement.
4. Distinguish verified facts, model estimates, and user preferences at a glance.
5. Optimize for meaningful signal, not metadata volume or notification count.
6. Use progressive disclosure for publishing and professional workflows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All core flows must be keyboard complete, work at 360 px width without horizontal overflow, support reduced motion, communicate state without relying on color alone, and provide touch targets appropriate for mobile use. Spanish copy, dates, numbers, units, and currency presentation must follow Argentine conventions while remaining clear to assistive technology.
